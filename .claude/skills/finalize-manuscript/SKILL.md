---
name: finalize-manuscript
description: Review a substantially complete manuscript for structural gaps, continuity problems, unresolved promises, chapter order, and revision priorities before final polish.
argument-hint: "[optional priorities such as continuity, pacing, or ending]"
---

Finalize the manuscript with priorities from `$ARGUMENTS`.

1. Synchronize state with `./sync-state.sh`.
2. Confirm which chapters actually exist and identify incomplete or unusually short files.
3. Use `continuity-editor` for a manuscript-wide continuity audit.
4. Use `smart-planner` for structural and pacing review.
5. Use `character-developer` when arc resolution or knowledge state is uncertain.
6. Produce a prioritized revision plan before making broad edits.
7. If the user requested edits, apply them in bounded passes and re-check affected continuity.
8. Do not declare the manuscript publication-ready solely because automated checks pass.

Summarize blocking issues, important revisions, optional polish, and what appears complete.
